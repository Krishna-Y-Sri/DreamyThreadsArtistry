Our Pricing & Design Options

Every brushstroke tells your story — crafted with care, creativity, and precision.

Transparent & Flexible Pricing

At Dreamy Threads Artistry, we believe wearable art should be accessible, transparent, and tailored to your imagination. Every garment is hand-painted with care, using premium paints and fabrics to ensure durability and comfort. Our pricing is designed to give you flexibility — whether you want us to source a branded shirt or kurti for you, or prefer to provide your own garment.

With Shirt/Kurti Option: We purchase the branded garment of your choice and add your selected design. Delivery charges are included in the sample totals.
Without Shirt/Kurti Option: You provide a suitable garment, and we handle the painting and return delivery. This option is more economical if you already have a favorite piece.

Each design category — Basic, Custom, and Detailed — reflects the complexity and size of the artwork. From simple motifs and quotes to large, multi-element illustrations, we ensure every brushstroke is crafted with precision. Please note that the prices shown are indicative samples. Final quotations may vary depending on brand, size, fabric, artwork complexity, and delivery location.
